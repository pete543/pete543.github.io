Production direction

Requirements for a production portal

A deployed employee system would place verified identity, role-based permissions, encrypted data, audit logging, retention rules, and administrative ownership ahead of convenience.

  • Company-managed identity and multifactor authentication
  • Least-privilege access by employee role
  • Centralized, encrypted client records
  • Documented ownership and data quality controls
  • Security review before real information is migrated
