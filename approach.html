Consulting Approach

How PCP works with a client

The method changes with the project, but the basic steps stay the same.

How we work

Four steps used on each engagement

The work starts with the client’s problem and ends with a documented handoff.

Listen

Meet with the people involved and document the problem, limits, and expected result.

Plan

Set the scope, schedule, responsibilities, reporting method, and approval process.

Manage

Track the work, record decisions, resolve issues, and report changes.

Handoff

Confirm what was delivered and give the client the records needed to maintain it.

Our principles

Project information should be easy to find

A current schedule, decision log, risk list, and responsibility list give the team one record of the project.

  • Start with the business problem, not a preferred tool
  • Make ownership and decisions visible
  • Communicate for the audience, not the template
  • Surface risks while there is still time to act
  • Leave the client stronger than we found them
