Usability Testing & Auditing

Watch people use the system. Fix what gets in their way.

PCP tests real tasks, records the problems, and gives the project team a ranked list of changes.

Evidence over assumption

See the experience through the user’s work.

A system can meet its technical requirements and still make everyday tasks unnecessarily difficult. Structured evaluation reveals where expectations, workflows, and interfaces fall out of alignment.

PCP provides a neutral view of what is working, what is getting in the way, and which changes deserve attention first.

Questions we help answer

  • Can users complete priority tasks?
  • Where do they hesitate or make errors?
  • Does the workflow match the real process?
  • Which findings create the greatest risk?
  • What should the team improve first?
Evaluation services

Focused insight at any stage.

Review

Expert usability audit

Assess workflows, navigation, consistency, feedback, error prevention, and clarity against established principles.

Observe

Task-based testing

Watch representative users attempt realistic activities and capture patterns, barriers, and questions.

Listen

Stakeholder interviews

Gather operational context from users, support teams, process owners, sponsors, and technical partners.

Trace

Workflow assessment

Follow tasks across systems and handoffs to expose duplication, ambiguity, and avoidable delays.

Prioritize

Findings roadmap

Rank issues by user impact, business risk, frequency, and implementation effort.

Verify

Improvement validation

Retest revised experiences to confirm that changes address the original problem.
