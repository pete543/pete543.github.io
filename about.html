About PCP

A Las Vegas consultancy founded in 2015.

PCP is a 20-person team of PMI-certified project consultants led by Joanne Schmidt and Alexis Green.

Our story

A shared idea, thirty years in the making.

Joanne Schmidt and Alexis Green founded Project Consultants Plus in Las Vegas in February 2015 after building more than 30 years of combined software-development project management experience.

They saw an opportunity to give organizations focused access to senior project leadership—people who could understand the technology, communicate with stakeholders, and keep delivery grounded in business reality.

Today, PCP coordinates a network of 20 consultants and supports engagements in the United States and abroad.
